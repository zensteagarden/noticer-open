# Why I’m building Noticer

AI and automation can say “done” before the result anyone needed has actually happened.

I’m building Noticer around a simple question: what would we need to observe before believing that claim?

The work starts with the intended result, makes the success condition explicit, and separates evidence integrity from evidence of success. Missing evidence stays unresolved. The original claimant does not get to decide the verifier’s verdict.

I’m opening the public verifier and protocol so other builders can inspect the disclosed rules, try synthetic failures, and build evidence exporters and receipt viewers. The private investigation system stays separate. The current public release checks supplied packets and exact-text evidence; it does not observe a live destination or prove that an external action occurred.

I’ll share more of the build story as the project develops: what failed, what changed, and what the evidence actually supports.

## Hire me to examine one workflow

If you run an automation and need a clearer answer than a green dashboard, I can help:

- define the specific result your workflow is supposed to produce;
- identify what evidence would establish that result and what it would leave unknown;
- inspect an agreed workflow using access and evidence you authorize;
- document supported findings, missing evidence, and a prioritized repair and recheck plan.

The first engagement is deliberately small: **one workflow, one intended outcome, one written evidence report**. Scope, price, access, and delivery date are agreed before work starts. Live checks depend on authorized access to the relevant systems. I do not promise that every claim can be verified or that every workflow has a defect.
